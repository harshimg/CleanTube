About CleanTube

CleanTube is a privacy-first, minimal YouTube ad blocker designed to protect your viewing experience with zero bloated trackers and minimal host permissions.

  • 🔒 No data collection: We do not collect, transmit, or monetize your viewing habits.
  • ⚡ Lightweight: Powered by Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest and CSS acceleration.
  • 🎯 Targeted permissions: Only requests permission for *.youtube.com.
