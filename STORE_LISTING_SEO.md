## 📄 Detailed Description (Store Listing Body)

```text
Stream clean, uninterrupted videos with CleanTube — the smart ad blocker designed specifically for YouTube™.

CleanTube removes video ads, preroll commercials, companion banners, sponsored cards, and popups before they even start playing, giving you a seamless YouTube Premium-like experience.

✓ Why Choose CleanTube:
Unlike generic ad blockers that demand access to read and change your personal data across every website you visit (<all_urls>), CleanTube strictly requests permissions only on youtube.com. Your passwords, browsing history, and personal data remain 100% private.

✓ Core Features:
• Blocks pre-roll, mid-roll, and post-roll video advertisements automatically
• Instant auto-skip on unskippable ads with zero delay
• Removes banner ads, image overlays, and sponsored video cards
• Eliminates empty black boxes and layout gaps from your feeds
• Blocks advertisements on external websites that embed YouTube videos
• Fully compatible with live streams and YouTube Shorts
• Toggleable option to remove annotations and interactive end-cards
• Channel Whitelisting: Support your favorite creators by whitelisting them with a single click
• Real-time Statistics: Monitor your total ads blocked and watch time saved
• Speeds up video buffering and page load times

✓ How It Works:
CleanTube combines Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest rules with client-side player response sanitization. Ad network calls (Doubleclick, GoogleAdServices) and ad placement configurations are pruned before the video player initializes. No black loading screens, no 5-second countdowns, and no anti-adblock detection warnings.

✓ Lightweight & 100% Free:
Free to download and use. Built with modern Manifest V3 standards for maximum speed, minimal battery draw, and zero memory bloat.
```
