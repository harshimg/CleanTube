# Privacy Policy for CleanTube — Ad Blocker for YouTube™

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

CleanTube ("we", "our", or "extension") is an ad-blocking browser extension designed specifically for YouTube™. We are firmly committed to user privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy describes how CleanTube handles user information.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
CleanTube does **not** collect, store, record, transmit, or monitor any personal data or browsing activity.
- We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or location data.
- We do not track your browsing history or YouTube viewing habits.
- We do not log video URLs, search queries, or account information.
- We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry services.

---

### 2. Permissions & How They Are Used
CleanTube strictly requests permissions required to block ads and improve playback performance:

- **`storage`**: Used solely to save your local extension settings (e.g., custom toggles, dark mode preferences, total blocked ad counter, and creator whitelist entries) directly inside your browser's local memory. This data never leaves your device.
- **`declarativeNetRequest`**: Used to instruct the browser engine to block network requests communicating with external ad servers and tracking pixels (such as DoubleClick and GoogleAdServices).
- **`scripting` & `webNavigation`**: Used to initialize player sanitization immediately when a YouTube video page loads, stripping ad placements prior to playback.
- **`activeTab`**: Used only when clicking the CleanTube popup toolbar button to detect the current YouTube channel handle for the whitelist feature.
- **`alarms`**: Used to schedule a local reset of the daily "Blocked Today" ad counter at midnight.
- **Host Permission (`*.youtube.com`)**: Scoped strictly to YouTube to manipulate the video player state, auto-skip commercial interruptions, and collapse empty ad grid slots. CleanTube has zero access to any other website you visit.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
Because CleanTube does not collect any user data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any information to third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or commercial entities under any circumstances.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
CleanTube does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
If we update our extension or modify permissions in future versions, this policy will be updated accordingly.
